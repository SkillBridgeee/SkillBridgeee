## Completeness

We first evaluated the depth and complexity of the main epics in your app, along with their contribution to the app, the tangible value they provide to the user, and their alignment with the app’s goals.

We evaluated the extent to which your app meets the course requirements articulated at the start of the semester, and whether they are implemented effectively, they integrate seamlessly, and are indeed essential to the app.

We also looked at the robustness and completeness of the different features you implemented: are all the features finished and polished, are they secure and bug-free, and are they thoughtfully designed.

In general, your definition of epic seems a bit off, we think you misunderstand epics for user stories. However the app covers the main flows planned, so it's good on this side.

You meet the minimal requirements of the course in term of features, but there are a few issues that makes them feel uncomplete:

- When trying to delete a conversation, it fails and shows an error, and I can only delete a conversation by spamming the button.

- On the map, when clicking on a booking the cards appear in the center of the screen and follow the screen, not staying attached to the location pin, which makes the feature feel incomplete.

- Also my phone had some struggle getting the location (which is not your fault) however signing in and creating a booking is annoying without a location because you need one that is valid. The issue is also that it doesn't display an error when having no location but prevent you from creating a booking, so you have to "guess" that the address is not valid and you need to find a valid one by hand.


For this part, you received 5.25/6.

We then evaluated the performance and reliability of the final product, i.e., the APK: we wanted to see that your APK is stable and the UI responds quickly and has seamless navigation.

Next we looked into your implementation of user authentication and multi-user support: does the app correctly manage users, can users personalize their accounts, does the app support session persistence, are multi-user interactions well supported, can accounts be used on another device, and is account information preserved when switching devices.

There are a few features that could be "broken" with some actions: when deleting my account, I stay on the app, but my informations are wiped out. ## User Experience

For this part, we wanted to see how intuitive and user-friendly the app is for real users. Beyond having good usability, did you pay attention to streamlining the interactions, is it easy to figure out, can new users start making good use of the app quickly, are the interaction flows well thought out and refined.

In general the app is usable, and provide a good experience. But the app can be a bit hard to understand for new users and some features lack a bit of robustness. There were a few issues we found that could make the app feel a bit "unprofessional" or hurt the user experience:

- Sometimes the screens take a bit of time to transition, because of that if you click too fast it registers the click on the older screen and navigate in something completly different that what you wanted to do which can be confusing.

- When booking for a decimal number of hours, I can get a price of for example 10.83333333333332 which doesn't really make sense given there is no unit lower than cents.

- There is no confirmation for deleting a conversation, if there were no failures, you could delete a conversation by misclick, which could be an issue.

- Requests are just always under proposals, so it can be annoying and unintuitive to have to scroll all the way down to get to the requests

- For selecting the time and date of a booking the process isn't really user friendly. It takes time to input one date and time and you need two. Maybe a date and a duration would be simpler to input.


For this part, you received 4.2/6.

## Design and Maintainability

We evaluated whether your code is of high quality and employs best practices.
We expect the codebase to be polished, well documented, follow consistent conventions, be modular, and allow for easy modifications.
You should be able to employ advanced techniques by now, such as asynchronous functions (flows, coroutines), good resource management, and automated dependency injection (e.g., with Hilt).

We also assessed your overall app architecture and design, looking in particular at aspects surrounding robustness and scalability.
We looked at both the codebase and the documentation of the app (Wiki and architecture diagram), we expect your design to demonstrate thoughtful consideration for performance, maintainability, and future growth.

The architecture of the app is good. There were still issues with the scalability of tests. Also making more composable components and reusing them would make the development process faster and probably also the app more robust.

Some code (including the tests) sometimes hard to read and maintain because not modular enough. Also there were some issue with the test doing something else than what the name suggest, if you modify the test flow, be sure to also modify the name to reflect what it really does.

Also we noted that there were a few instances of PRs pushing commented out code/tests/dependencies to main, be sure to avoid that! There was also a bit of code duplication that was hard to justify pushed on main.


For this part, you received 4.7/6.

## QA (Testing)

The first aspect we looked at here was your test suite, in terms of both quality and the final line coverage.
We expect the testing to be rigorous and to cover all components and edge cases, and they should validate every significant user journey.
Your end-to-end tests should be detailed and include error-handling scenarios.
The tests should be well-documented and easy to maintain.
Finally, your test suite should demonstrate advanced techniques, mock data for performance testing, and automated regression tests.

You have good coverage, quantity is definetly here, but your tests lack quality. You probably used AI to generate tests, which is a good use case for AI, we don't deny that, but it needs to be controlled. The tests are a bit inconsistent in quality, some are just happy tests, they cover flows, but don't actually assert enough so the test really fail even if just a small detail stopped working.


For this part, you received 5.07/6.

We also assessed your use of Figma and the architecture diagram for effective UI design, organization, and app structure planning.
By this stage, we expect your Figma and Architecture diagram to be complete and up-to-date. The Figma should be consistent with the UI and the Architecture diagram should be comprehensive.
